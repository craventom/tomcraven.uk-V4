A little about this portfolio

Made to hold a feeling.

I photograph landscapes, streets, nights out and wedding days. I’m drawn to photographs that hold a feeling as much as a scene — the changing light, the people in a place, and the small moments you might otherwise miss.

This is a growing collection of work from the Wirral and wherever the camera takes me.
